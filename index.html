Trainer: Strength & Sleep

Strength, easy runs and sleep, on iPhone and Apple Watch.

What it does

  • Every month is a project: goals proposed from last month, progress all month, and a month-over-month report.
  • Two full-body strength sessions, at home (bodyweight and a band, with levels) or in the gym (automatic weight progression).
  • Easy runs kept under your heart-rate ceiling, and a monthly heart-rate recovery test at a fixed pace.
  • Workouts run on Apple Watch, with sets, rest timer and heart rate; the iPhone follows live.
  • A daily readiness score from last night's sleep, HRV and resting heart rate.
  • Demo videos for every exercise, reminders, personal records and a full export of your data.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your devices.
