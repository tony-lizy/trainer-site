Privacy Policy

Trainer: Strength & Sleep · Effective 22 November 2026

In short

Trainer doesn't collect your data. It has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. Everything it reads or records stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch, unless you choose to share an export or turn on the optional AI analysis with your own Claude API key.

Apple Health data

With your permission, Trainer reads data from Apple Health, such as workouts, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep, weight, body fat percentage, lean body mass, VO₂ max, height and waist circumference and, when you export, the other Health types you allow. It writes your strength and run workouts, with heart rate and active energy, back to Apple Health.

  • Health data is used only inside the app: to plan and log your training, and to show your scorecards, readiness, records, history, your monthly project's goals (including body goals such as weight and body fat) and your fitness checks. A visceral-fat level, waist and grip you enter by hand are stored with your settings on the iPhone.
  • Health data is never sent to us, never used for advertising or marketing, and never sold. It reaches anyone else only when you share an export yourself or turn on the AI analysis (below).
  • You can change what Trainer may read or write at any time in the Health app (Sharing › Apps › Trainer) or in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices.

Data stored by the app

Your programme settings, logged sessions and sets are stored on your iPhone, and the watch keeps today's plan and unsent workouts until they reach the phone. The iPhone and Apple Watch exchange this data directly through Apple's WatchConnectivity. If you use iCloud Backup or an encrypted computer backup, the app's data is included in that backup under Apple's terms.

Exports

The Export screen creates a file with your Trainer data and, if you choose, your Apple Health data. It is created on your iPhone and stays there (it's not included in iCloud Backup) until you replace or delete it. It leaves your device only when you share it yourself, for example to Files, Mail or AirDrop. Once shared, it is handled by the app or person you shared it with.

AI analysis (optional, off by default)

The Analysis tab can write a weekly report with Claude, a model made by Anthropic, using a Claude API key you create in your own Anthropic account. Nothing is sent until you add your key and turn the analysis on.

  • What is sent: summaries of the last 7, 30 and 90 days (sessions planned and done, workout minutes, strength trends and records, HRR test results, sleep times and durations, average resting heart rate and HRV, weight, body fat and lean mass, fitness check results, readiness and sleep debt) and your programme settings. No raw Health samples, names or other identifiers.
  • Who receives it: Anthropic, directly from your iPhone, under your API account and Anthropic's Privacy Policy. It never passes through us, and usage is billed to your Anthropic account.
  • Your key is stored only in your iPhone's Keychain. Reports are stored only on your iPhone. Removing the key or turning the analysis off stops all sending.
  • Reports are general training and sleep feedback, not medical advice.

Exercise demo videos

Exercise pages can play a demo video from YouTube, using YouTube's embedded player in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). The player loads only when you tap a video. YouTube then receives the usual information any web request carries, such as your IP address and device type, and handles it under Google's Privacy Policy. Trainer sends YouTube nothing else: none of your training or Health data.

Notifications and Live Activities

Reminders (workout, bedtime, morning plan and the weekly review, all off by default) and workout Live Activities are created on your device. Nothing about them is sent to a server.

Children

Trainer is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly handle children's data in any way beyond what is described here.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
