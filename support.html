Common questions

The watch doesn't start when I tap Start on the iPhone. Open Trainer on the watch once, keep the watch unlocked and near the phone, then try again. You can also start any workout on the iPhone only.

Readiness says "Not enough data". It needs last night's sleep, or HRV and resting heart rate from Apple Watch. Wear the watch to bed, and allow Trainer to read Sleep and Heart Rate Variability in Health.

My workouts from other apps don't count. Allow Trainer to read Workouts in the Health app (Sharing › Apps › Trainer).

An exercise is too easy or too hard. Open it (the ⓘ next to it) and tap Easier or Harder under Your level. Levels also move up by themselves after 15+ reps on every set.

My heart-rate recovery test says "not valid". After the watch buzzes STOP, stand completely still for the full 2 minutes and don't touch the watch. Walking, or heart rate rising after the stop, makes the test invalid so it can't distort your trend.

How do I get all my data out? Settings › Export data: pick a range, keep Apple Health data on, Create export, then Share. The zip contains CSV files and a README explaining each one.

Where is my data stored? Only on your iPhone and Apple Watch. See the Privacy Policy.
